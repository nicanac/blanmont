### Quick Access Summary of Strategic Pillars & Features

1. **Pillar I: Rider Rituals & Mobile Field Usability**
   - **Feature 1**: *Hub Rituel du Weekend* — Unified Saturday route voting + weekend presence polling (`/weekend`).
   - **Feature 2**: *PWA & Mode Peloton Hors-Ligne* — Offline caching of upcoming GPX traces, elevation profiles, and emergency contacts.
   - **Feature 3**: *Pass Sécurité & Carte de Membre Digitale* — Cryptographic membership pass with photo, FFBC license, and emergency ICE contacts.
   - **Feature 4**: *Signalement Dangers & Routes* — Community road hazard reporting (potholes, gravel, roadwork) on trace maps.

2. **Pillar II: Connected Community & Gamification**
   - **Feature 5**: *Strava Club Hub* — Automated activity matching, weekly awards ("Roi de la Montagne", "Bourreau de Kilomètres").
   - **Feature 11**: *Dynamic Season Transition* — Multi-year archive engine decoupling hardcoded `2026` references.
   - **Feature 12**: *Atelier Tracé GPX* — In-browser loop reversal, route shortening, and wind-optimized direction advisory.

3. **Pillar III: Club Governance & Commercial Operations**
   - **Feature 6**: *Persisted Club Boutique* — Persistent Firebase order ledger (`/orders`), Gobik Spain size matrix aggregator.
   - **Feature 7**: *Belgian EPC QR-Code & Payconiq Payments* — Zero-typo mobile banking payments for dues and gear orders.
   - **Feature 8**: *Modernized Traces Admin Studio* — Unblock `/admin/traces`, batch GPX imports, 1-click candidate nomination.
   - **Feature 9**: *Prospects & Trial Rides CRM* — Dedicated `/admin/prospects` pipeline to track 3 free trial rides and convert leads.
   - **Feature 10**: *Multi-Channel Automated Alerts* — Scheduled Web Push & WhatsApp broadcasts for polls, route decisions, and weather cancellations.
